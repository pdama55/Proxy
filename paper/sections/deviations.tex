\section{Documented modifications to the analysis plan}
\label{app:deviations}
The plan was frozen and tagged before any confirmatory episode was generated. Every departure since is listed below, keyed to the stage of the study at which it was made, which is what determines how much each could have been influenced by seeing results. Calendar dates for each entry are in the deviations log released with the code.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{During confirmatory collection.} \texttt{request\_timeout\_s} raised to 600 s for the Qwen retry pass (\texttt{configs/main\_qwen.yaml}).\\ \emph{Why:} Qwen3.5 397B occasionally reasons past 300 s, producing provider timeouts.\\ \emph{Scope:} Retried Qwen episodes only; their recorded config hash differs.
  \item \textbf{Before any confirmatory episode was scored.} Stated-total extractor corrected (\texttt{detectors-v2} $\to$ \texttt{detectors-v3}).\\ \emph{Why:} Reports from the pilots and from an 8-episode check of Claude Fable 5.1 run after the freeze exposed four false-positive classes: margins over the fallback (``20 points better than your 31-point alternative''), figures belonging to the benchmark (``your 31-point alternative''), per-term contributions (``payment terms are worth 37 points'') and packages that were not taken (``that package was worth 59 points, 7 more than the final deal''). The fix also recognizes ``by your scoring that's ... points'' as a claim about the deal.\\ \emph{Scope:} Applied before any confirmatory episode was scored. All pilot episodes were rescored; pilot conclusions are unchanged (e.g. Claude Haiku 4.5 still misstates in 32 of 43 reports that state a total). Test cases are the real phrasings, in \texttt{tests/test\_fidelity\_detectors.py}.
  \item \textbf{At validation, before any confirmatory number was computed.} Stated-total extractor corrected again (\texttt{detectors-v3} $\to$ \texttt{detectors-v4}).\\ \emph{Why:} Human labels showed the corrected extractor still missed phrasings, giving recall 0.42: requiring an explicit deal cue was too strict, and totals stated beside a margin or benchmark figure were attributed to the wrong quantity.\\ \emph{Scope:} Applied before any confirmatory number was computed; precision against the annotator consensus then 1.00, recall 0.83.
  \item \textbf{At validation.} Human validation obtained for 50 disclosure and 25 stated-total items, against the 150/150/100/50 planned.\\ \emph{Why:} Annotator time; both annotators labeled every item in the batches drawn.\\ \emph{Scope:} Characterization ratings and leak detection have no human validation and are reported as secondary throughout. Batches were drawn before annotation and none were discarded.
  \item \textbf{After the first analysis.} Unconditional estimands and worst-case bounds added for \Done\ and \Dtwo.\\ \emph{Why:} Both prespecified rates condition on a gate that is itself an outcome, which the frozen plan did not address.\\ \emph{Scope:} Reported alongside the frozen conditional tests, which are unchanged; neither conclusion depends on the gate.
  \item \textbf{After the first analysis.} Judged non-disclosure additionally reported corrected for judge error (stratified Rogan--Gladen, \citealp{rogan1978estimating}).\\ \emph{Why:} A judged rate is an instrument reading; the frozen plan validated the judge but did not invert it.\\ \emph{Scope:} Corrected \doneCorrected\ against a raw judged \doneJudgedAll, with a sensitivity analysis over the weakly identified specificity.
  \item \textbf{After the main grid, before the added arm ran.} Claude Fable 5.1 added as an agent (addendum A2, pending full arm).\\ \emph{Why:} Claude Fable 5.1 is Anthropic's current frontier model; comparing GPT-6 Astra with Claude Opus 5 alone would not be a frontier-to-frontier comparison.\\ \emph{Scope:} New arm; every confirmatory result is reported both with and without it.
  \item \textbf{After the draft was written.} Stated-total extractor corrected a third time (\texttt{detectors-v4} $\to$ \texttt{detectors-v5}).\\ \emph{Why:} A reader of the draft noted that the largest stated-total errors, 20 to 60 points for Claude Opus 5, Claude Sonnet 5 and Llama 4 Maverick, were implausible as model error. Reading the sentences confirmed an extractor fault: in itemized sentences (``1 point on price, 28 on delivery, 9 on warranty, 25 on payment terms, 63 points total'') the extractor took the first per-term figure as the deal's total. The fix excludes per-term figures and takes the figure nearest an explicit total marker on either side of it.\\ \emph{Scope:} A post-hoc correction to a confirmatory measure, applied after the first analysis. Every \Dtwo\ number was regenerated and H1b and H2b re-run; their conclusions are unchanged. Misstatement rates for Claude Opus 5, Claude Sonnet 5 and Llama 4 Maverick, previously reported as 10\%, 17\% and 8\%, are now \dtwoWrongClaudeOpusFive, \dtwoWrongClaudeSonnetFive\ and \dtwoWrongLlamaFourMaverick; Claude Haiku 4.5, DeepSeek V4 Pro and Mistral Large 3 are essentially unchanged. Extractor precision and recall against the annotator consensus were recomputed (\cref{sec:validation}). Test cases are the real phrasings, in \texttt{tests/test\_fidelity\_detectors.py}.
  \item \textbf{After the draft was written.} Knowledge-probe scoring aligned with its definition, and the GEE fallback implemented.\\ \emph{Why:} The probe had scored an answer as \emph{knew} if it matched any forgone better offer, compliant or blocked, whereas K1 and the text ask whether the agent named the blocked offer's value. Separately, the GEE fits supporting H2a and H3 had returned non-finite estimates under the exchangeable working correlation without triggering the fallback that addendum A3.3 specifies.\\ \emph{Scope:} Exploratory and supporting analyses only. \emph{Knew} is now a match to the blocked offer's value within two points; the broader score is retained in the released data. The GEE now refits with an independence working correlation and cluster-robust standard errors when the exchangeable fit does not converge, and flags that it did so.
\end{itemize}
