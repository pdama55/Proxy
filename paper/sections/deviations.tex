\section{Deviations from the preregistration}
\label{app:deviations}
The plan was frozen at git tag \texttt{prereg-v1} before any confirmatory episode was generated. Every departure since:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{2026-09-15.} \texttt{request\_timeout\_s} raised to 600 s for the Qwen retry pass (\texttt{configs/main\_qwen.yaml}) \emph{Why:} Qwen3.5 397B occasionally reasons past 300 s, producing provider timeouts \emph{Scope:} Retried Qwen episodes only; their recorded config hash differs
  \item \textbf{2026-09-15.} Stated-total extractor corrected (\texttt{detectors-v2} → \texttt{detectors-v3}) \emph{Why:} Pilot reports from Claude Fable 5.1 and others exposed four false-positive classes: margins over the fallback ("20 points better than your 31-point alternative"), figures belonging to the benchmark ("your 31-point alternative"), per-term contributions ("payment terms are worth 37 points") and packages that were not taken ("that package was worth 59 points, 7 more than the final deal"). The fix also recognises "by your scoring that's ... points" as a claim about the deal. \emph{Scope:} Applied before any confirmatory episode was scored. All pilot episodes were rescored; pilot conclusions are unchanged (e.g. Claude Haiku 4.5 still misstates in 32 of 43 reports that state a total). Test cases are the real phrasings, in \texttt{tests/test\_fidelity\_detectors.py}.
  \item \textbf{2026-09-15.} Stated-total extractor corrected again (\texttt{detectors-v3} → \texttt{detectors-v4}) \emph{Why:} Human labels showed the corrected extractor still missed phrasings, giving recall 0.42: requiring an explicit deal cue was too strict, and totals stated beside a margin or benchmark figure were attributed to the wrong quantity. \emph{Scope:} Applied before any confirmatory number was computed; precision against the annotator consensus then 1.00, recall 0.83.
  \item \textbf{2026-09-16.} Human validation obtained for 50 disclosure and 25 stated-total items, against the 150/150/100/50 planned. \emph{Why:} Annotator time; both annotators labelled every item in the batches drawn. \emph{Scope:} Characterization ratings and leak detection have no human validation and are reported as secondary throughout. Batches were drawn before annotation and none were discarded.
  \item \textbf{2026-09-16.} Unconditional estimands and worst-case bounds added for \Done\ and \Dtwo. \emph{Why:} Both preregistered rates condition on a gate that is itself an outcome, which the frozen plan did not address. \emph{Scope:} Reported alongside the frozen conditional tests, which are unchanged; neither conclusion depends on the gate.
  \item \textbf{2026-09-16.} Judged non-disclosure additionally reported corrected for judge error (stratified Rogan--Gladen). \emph{Why:} A judged rate is an instrument reading; the frozen plan validated the judge but did not invert it. \emph{Scope:} Corrected \doneCorrected\ against a raw judged \doneJudgedAll, with a sensitivity analysis over the weakly identified specificity.
  \item \textbf{2026-09-15.} Claude Fable 5.1 added as an agent (addendum A2, pending full arm) \emph{Why:} Claude Fable 5.1 is Anthropic's current frontier model; comparing GPT-6 Astra with Claude Opus 5 alone would not be a frontier-to-frontier comparison \emph{Scope:} New arm; reported with and without
\end{itemize}
