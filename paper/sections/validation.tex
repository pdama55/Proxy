\section{Measure validation}
\label{sec:validation}

Two measures involve judgment, so both were validated against two annotators who labelled the same stratified sample of 50 exposed episodes and 25 reports, independently, blind to model, condition, detector output and judge output, under guidelines fixed in advance.

\paragraph{Annotators agree on the decision the paper uses.} On the binary question---does this report convey that a better deal was blocked?---the annotators agreed on \kappaHumanRaw\ of items ($\kappa = \kappaHuman$). On the four-way label they agreed on only \kappaHumanFourWayRaw\ of items ($\kappa = \kappaHumanFourWay$): 20 of their 24 disagreements are \emph{mentioned but not acknowledged} versus \emph{absent}, that is, how to grade a report that failed, not whether it failed. The paper's measure is the binary one.

\paragraph{The judge matches the annotators.} Against the items on which the annotators agreed, the primary judge reached $\kappa = \kappaJudgePrimary$ (\kappaJudgePrimaryRaw\ raw agreement) on \kappaJudgePrimaryN\ items, and the second judge, from a different developer, $\kappa = \kappaJudgeSecond$ on the \kappaJudgeSecondN\ it covered. Both clear the prespecified demotion threshold of 0.6, so judged non-disclosure is reported as a confirmatory result.

\paragraph{Correcting the headline rate for judge error.} A judged rate is the truth seen through an imperfect instrument, so we invert the instrument rather than quote it raw. On the \validationN\ blocked-alternative items the annotators agreed on, the judge made no errors (\validationTP\ true positives, \validationTN\ true negative, \validationFP\ false positives, \validationFN\ false negatives), giving a sensitivity of \validationSens. Because the validation items were drawn stratified on whether the report mentions any other offer, we estimate the judge's error rates within stratum and recombine them at the population shares, which is the Rogan--Gladen correction \citep{rogan1978estimating} applied stratum-wise. The corrected non-disclosure rate is \doneCorrected, against a raw judged \doneJudgedAll.

That agreement is not by itself strong evidence, because reports that do convey the blocked deal are rare and the validation set therefore contains only \validationNegatives\ of them: specificity is the weakly identified parameter, and a point estimate of 1.00 from so few negatives would claim a precision the labels do not support. The correction is, however, insensitive to it in the direction that matters. Holding sensitivity at its estimate and sweeping specificity down, the corrected rate is \doneCorrectedNineSpec\ at a specificity of 0.90 and still \doneCorrectedHalfSpec\ at 0.50---a judge wrong about half the time on the cases it is most likely to get wrong. No specificity consistent with the labels brings non-disclosure near a rate that would change the paper's conclusion.

\paragraph{The judge-free measures are floors.} Of the \stageOneN\ sampled reports that the judge-free pattern check called silent, the annotators judged none to convey the blocked deal (precision \stageOnePrecision). Independently of any judgment, \doneLowerAll\ of reports refer to no other offer or option at all, which bounds non-disclosure from below without using a judge.

\paragraph{The stated-total extractor.} The annotators recorded the point total each report states for its own deal, agreeing exactly on all 25 items. Against that consensus the extractor's precision is \extractorPrecision\ and its recall \extractorRecall: it never reads a wrong figure and misses about one claim in six, so the rate at which models state a value is itself a floor. The preregistration demotes \Dtwo\ below 0.9 precision; \Dtwo\ stands.

\paragraph{Limits.} The sample is 50 disclosure items and 25 stated-total reports out of \exposedCount\ exposed episodes, One annotator is an author and the other an unpaid volunteer with no involvement in the design, implementation or analysis. Labels were collected after judging, so they validate the instrument rather than adjudicate cases, and no reported result was changed because of a label. The extractor was corrected after the labels exposed phrasings it missed; that correction is recorded as a deviation and was applied before any confirmatory number was computed. Portrayal ratings and leak detection were not put to annotators and are reported as secondary throughout.
